# PaidPace — Founding Pilot Recruitment Kit

Status: Prepared; **do not publish invites until the live form is connected and tested.** No applicants or customers acquired yet.

## Offer
Free early-access pilot for freelancers and small-business owners who regularly send invoices, especially those tracking late payments manually. Pilot feature set is still in preparation; no promises of automated sending or successful collection.

## Recruitment guardrails
- No spending until $1,000 in monthly recurring revenue; payment-processing fees, if any, are charged only with transactions.
- Never send automated unsolicited bulk messages; personalize each contact and respect community rules.
- Do not request actual invoices, bank details, financial documents, customer names, or payment credentials.
- Do not describe the beta as usable until secure production services are deployed.
- Get consent to contact interested applicants; allow easy opt-outs.
- Verify the actual signup page works before sharing the application link.

## Target profile
Independent consultants, solo creative agencies, cleaners, landscapers, handypeople, and other service providers sending 5–100 invoices per month.

## Reddit feedback-first post
**Title:** Building a very simple overdue-invoice tracker — what would make it worth using?

I'm working on PaidPace, an early-stage tool for freelancers and small service businesses who track unpaid invoices in spreadsheets or email.

The current site is only an interactive design preview; the production app and pilot signup aren't ready yet. I'm trying to avoid building features nobody needs.

If you invoice clients, which part of following up on overdue payments is most frustrating? And what do your current tools already do well?

I'm not asking anyone to upload financial data or buy anything. Just looking for honest feedback from people who face the problem.

## Pilot interview questions
1. How many invoices do you usually send in a month?
2. Where do you track which invoices are still unpaid?
3. When an invoice becomes overdue, what do you do next?
4. How much time do you spend on follow-ups in a typical month?
5. What are you already paying for invoicing or bookkeeping software?
6. What's one thing you wish your current setup handled?
7. If PaidPace solved that problem, would $12/month feel reasonable? Why or why not?
8. Would you like to hear when free testing becomes available?
