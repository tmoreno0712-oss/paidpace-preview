Privacy notice

Last updated: November 3, 2026

PaidPace is an early-stage project. This notice describes the information handled through our public preview, free invoice reminder generator, and pilot interest form. A full customer invoicing application is not yet available.

Information you provide

When you choose to submit our founding-pilot interest form, we request your name, email address, business category, approximate monthly invoice count, current invoice-tracking method, and consent to contact you. If you add an optional message, we receive it too. Please don't send actual invoices, client names, payment-card numbers, bank details, or confidential business records.

How the information is used

We use submitted information to understand interest in PaidPace and contact you about the free pilot, related questions, or availability. We do not sell your form information. We do not use the interest form to charge you or enroll you in a paid subscription. We do not promise a specific launch date.

Service providers

The form sends submissions using Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service, which forwards notification emails to the project owner's mailbox. Our public pages are hosted on Render and our public website source is stored on GitHub. Those providers may process technical data such as IP addresses for normal service delivery and abuse prevention. Their own terms and privacy policies also apply.

Free generator

The invoice reminder generator operates in your browser. It does not send your selected reminder text to PaidPace, does not require an account, and does not deliver emails. The page host may still receive standard web-request and technical log information.

Storage and deletion

Pilot submission emails may remain in the project's mailbox until they are no longer necessary for the pilot or you ask us to delete them. We aim to honor unsubscribe or deletion requests and, where feasible, ask processors to remove corresponding records. The actual retention and deletion capabilities of external services also apply.

How to contact us or opt out

If you receive an email from PaidPace, reply to it to ask questions, stop further contact, or request deletion. If you have not yet received an email from us, you can contact us using the same public channel where you originally learned about PaidPace. We are working toward a dedicated privacy-contact option before the full product launches. We do not have a public support phone line.

Changes

We may revise this notice if the product, form provider, or business practices change. The updated version will be posted here with a new date. This privacy notice does not substitute for additional disclosures required for any future paid, production service.
